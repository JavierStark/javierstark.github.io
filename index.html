Tokenized digital bond platform on Canton Network (UMA Blockchain Technologies × ioBuilders)

Final project of the University of Málaga Blockchain Technologies course (run by NICS Lab; 18 students in 6 teams mentored by industry partners), built with ioBuilders.

DamlCanton NetworkGo.NET AspireDockerOpenTelemetryPrometheusGrafana
Code
See details

A Dockerized Canton network running a CIP-056 tokenized bond contract in Daml, a Go REST backend over the gRPC Ledger API, a web frontend to issue, transfer, burn and settle bonds with DvP allocations, a ledger event listener and a full observability stack.

Owned the Canton, blockchain and Daml layer, the system architecture, .NET Aspire orchestration and telemetry.

  • Designed the architecture and built the Canton/Daml layer of a tokenized bond platform on a local Canton network (sequencer, mediator, synchronizer, three participants) with a CIP-056 Daml contract and issue, transfer, burn and DvP settlement flows exposed through a Go REST backend over the gRPC Ledger API.
  • Orchestrated the platform with .NET Aspire and instrumented it with OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Tempo and Grafana; the bond lifecycle is covered by Playwright end-to-end tests.
  • Led to a Bachelor's thesis on Canton Network developed in collaboration with ioBuilders.
